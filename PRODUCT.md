# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and engineering hiring managers evaluating Dominik for full-stack software-development roles. Potential clients and collaborators are a secondary audience, followed by fellow developers and Dominik's professional network. Visitors need to understand his technical depth quickly, inspect credible examples of his work, and decide whether to begin a conversation.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Dominik's experience and selected work to position him for full-stack software-development opportunities where design judgment matters. Success means a recruiter reviews his projects or downloads his resume with confidence in both his engineering expertise and his ability to make thoughtful product decisions.

## Positioning

A full-stack engineer who combines deep technical execution with unusually strong design judgment.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: View selected work.
- Secondary CTA: Download resume.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Full-stack engineering depth, sharpened by design judgment."
- Belief ladder: Dominik is a technically strong full-stack engineer; he brings real product and design judgment; his work demonstrates finished outcomes and measurable impact; he is worth contacting for the role.
- Proof on hand: Finished personal, university, and client products; GitHub activity; and client or employer logos from freelance work. Supporting assets belong in `.impeccable/assets/proof/`.

## Brand Personality

Professionalism, expertise, and taste. The portfolio should create confidence in Dominik's engineering depth and leave visitors impressed by his design judgment. Its voice is direct, composed, and specific.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS styling, flashy agency effects, dense resume-like walls of text, and excessive animation. The site should not trade clarity or evidence for spectacle.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with engineering credibility; let design judgment sharpen the evidence.
2. Show finished work and outcomes instead of relying on claims.
3. Make the path from first impression to selected work immediate.
4. Express personality through precise details and deliberate interaction, not decoration.
5. Keep every experience clear, fast, and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Provide full keyboard access, semantic markup, strong color contrast, visible focus states, and reduced-motion alternatives without diminishing access to content or functionality.
